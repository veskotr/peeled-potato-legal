Privacy Policy for Peeled Potato

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Peeled Potato is a private peer-to-peer messaging application that lets paired users exchange messages and files between their devices.

Information we collect

Peeled Potato does not require an account and does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

The app does not use advertising or analytics services.

Technical information may be processed temporarily when devices establish a connection. This can include network addresses, connection identifiers, and WebRTC signalling information needed to connect paired devices.

Messages and files

Messages and files are transmitted over encrypted connections between paired devices. Peeled Potato does not operate a server that stores or reads the contents of users' messages or transferred files.

Content stored locally on a device remains under the control of that device's user.

Device pairing

Users pair devices using the in-app QR-code pairing process. Pairing and cryptographic identity information may be stored locally so that paired devices can recognise each other.

PIN protection

If the user enables PIN protection, the PIN and related security information are stored locally on the device and are not sent to Peeled Potato servers.

Infrastructure providers

Peeled Potato may use third-party infrastructure providers to operate temporary signalling or networking services needed to establish peer-to-peer connections. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses as part of normal network operation, security, and service delivery.

Data retention

Peeled Potato does not maintain user accounts or centrally store message history. Temporary technical information is retained only as needed to provide the connection service, subject to any operational or security logging performed by infrastructure providers.

Data sharing and sale

Peeled Potato does not sell personal information. User content is exchanged only with the recipient selected and paired by the user.

Children

Peeled Potato is not designed for children under the age of 16.

Security

Peeled Potato uses encrypted communications and device-to-device identity verification designed to protect communication between paired users. No method of electronic communication or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Peeled Potato's features or data-handling practices change. The latest version will always show its current update date.
